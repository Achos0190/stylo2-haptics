How the pieces connect

A OnePlus Pad 3 with the Stylo 2. Pen input reaches apps through standard Android. Haptic commands travel a separate route through OnePlus's own service.

Input is standard. Feedback is not.

Position, pressure and tilt reach every app as a MotionEvent. Android has no standard route from an app to a pen's motor.

Where Android haptics go

A system service holds the pen link.

On the Pad 3, com.oplus.ipemanager owns the Bluetooth connection. Apps bind to it through Binder, and they need a permission ordinary apps can't hold.

The vendor-service pattern

The pen plays the texture itself.

An app picks a tool texture and turns feedback on or off. The pen vibrates while the tip touches the glass. Windows uses the same model with standard HID waveforms.
